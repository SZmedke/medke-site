Frequently Asked Questions
MOQ · OEM · Certifications · Delivery · Warranty
01

Which brands are compatible?

90%+ of mainstream monitors: Philips, GE, Mindray, Drager, Fukuda, Nihon Kohden, Bionet, Aeon, etc.

02

Do you support OEM / ODM?

Yes — custom connectors, cable length, logo, packaging and instruction cards are all available.

03

What certifications do you hold?

CE, FDA, ISO 13485, TUV, MDR-compliant. Documentation provided with each order.

04

What is the MOQ? Can I mix?

100 pcs for stock models; mixed models in one order allowed; lower MOQ for OEM trials.

05

How long is delivery?

3–7 days for stock items; 15–25 days for OEM production; express shipping available.

06

What is the warranty?

12-month warranty on all products; defective items replaced free of charge.
